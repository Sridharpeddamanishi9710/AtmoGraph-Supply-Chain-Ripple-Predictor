

\# AtmoGraph - Dataset Sources and Selection



\## 1. Supply Chain Analysis Dataset



\*\*File:\*\* supply\_chain\_data.csv



\*\*Source:\*\* https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/harshsingh2209/supply-chain-analysis



\*\*Purpose:\*\*

\- Analyze product and supplier information.

\- Explore inventory and manufacturing details.

\- Study shipping and transportation information.

\- Identify potential supply-chain relationships.



\## 2. Port Disruption Database



\*\*File:\*\* Port\_Disruption\_database-master.zip



\*\*Source:\*\* https://github.com/jasperverschuur/Port\_Disruption\_database



\*\*Purpose:\*\*

\- Study historical port disruption events.

\- Analyze port shutdown and recovery durations.

\- Explore vessel activity data.

\- Identify port-related disruption features.



\## 3. Global Supply Chain Disruption Dataset



\*\*File:\*\* Global-Supply-Chain-Disruption-Dataset-2015-2026.zip



\*\*Source:\*\* https://github.com/kuldeepjang/Global-Supply-Chain-Disruption-Dataset-2015-2026-



\*\*Purpose:\*\*

\- Explore global trade routes and route operations.

\- Analyze geopolitical disruption information.

\- Study commodity and country-level information.

\- Identify features for transportation network analysis.



\## 4. Planned Data Processing



The selected datasets will be inspected individually to understand their schemas, columns, data types, missing values and relationships.



The relevant information will then be prepared for supply-chain graph construction and GNN feature engineering.
